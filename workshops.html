Workshops

Purpose

I have been conducting workshops during semester breaks to augment conventional curriculum-based learning by exposition of essential technical topics, keeping an emphasis on project development with a "learning by doing" approach.

Salient Features

  • Programming rigor: Solving problems by writing programs.
  • Content: Latest topics not covered in regular curriculum.
  • Practical hands-on: Emphasis on project development.
  • Delivery: Head start introduction through a tutorial-style exposition of concepts.

Target Audience

Students in their B.Tech., M.C.A., or M.Tech. programs.

Duration

Conducted mostly during the Summer Vacation (June–July). Students are expected to attend regularly for the initial few days; thereafter, they are allowed to develop projects remotely.
